## Common production methods

The available products and suitable artwork depend on the print process and substrate. These are common pairings, not guarantees for every supplier:

- **Inkjet / wide-format inkjet:** photo prints, posters, fine-art prints, banners, and many rigid or roll materials.
- **Electrophotographic (toner) printing:** short-run cards, flyers, brochures, books, and other paper goods.
- **UV digital printing:** rigid panels and selected promotional products; substrate compatibility and surface preparation matter.
- **Dye-sublimation:** polyester textiles and specially coated hard goods such as some drinkware or panels.
- **Direct-to-garment (DTG):** full-color designs printed directly onto compatible garments.
- **Direct-to-film (DTF) and heat-transfer printing:** transfer graphics applied to compatible apparel and textiles.
- **Digital label and packaging presses:** short-run labels and packaging, often with variable designs or data.
- **Direct-to-object printing:** selected three-dimensional products; printable area, shape, and material compatibility vary.
- **Digital embellishment:** selective varnish, foil effects, or raised textures, depending on equipment and substrate.

## Product data checklist

When documenting a product, include the details that help someone compare and order it:

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Clear product name and category |
| Variants | Sizes, shapes, colors, page counts, styles, or device compatibility |
| Substrate | Paper, fabric, vinyl, board, ceramic, or other material |
| Print method | Known process, if specified by the producer |
| Artwork | Accepted file types, template or dieline, bleed, resolution, color profile |
| Printed sides | One-sided, two-sided, wraparound, or placement options |
| Finish | Coating, laminate, texture, binding, edge treatment, or hardware |
| Output | What the buyer receives, including assembly, packaging, and quantity |
| Personalization | Names, photos, variable data, or other customer-specific options |
| Intended use | Indoor/outdoor, display, apparel, packaging, or other use, if specified |
| Source | Reliable product or supplier link and date last checked |
